Install

It's a website that lives on your home screen.

No app store, no account required. Add it once and it opens full-screen like any app, works offline, and remembers your plan.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open Unstuck in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with the arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open Unstuck in Chrome.
  2. Tap Add on the banner, or the ⋮ menu → Install app.
  3. Find it with your other apps.
Questions

The short version.

Is this for people with an ADHD diagnosis?

It's for anyone who freezes at the start of things or loses track of how many days are left. Diagnosed or not. If "I know what to do, I just can't start" sounds familiar, it's for you.

Why fixed minute options instead of typing a number?

Because choosing between 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 takes a second. Estimating "how long will this take?" from scratch is exactly the kind of decision that stops people from starting.

What happens if I miss three days?

You open the app and it shows today. That's it. Nothing turns red, nothing counts your missed days, nothing resets.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Sync is off unless you turn it on with your email; then your plans are stored in an account only you can read. No ads, no tracking, no selling anything about you.

Does the timer keep going if I switch apps?

Yes — it counts from the moment you tapped, so coming back later shows the right time or the "you're allowed to stop" message. On iPhone, the buzz at zero only plays if Unstuck is on screen; that's a limitation of web apps on iOS we're working around.

What does it cost?

Nothing right now. If it ever does, the countdown, timers, and "I'm stuck" will stay free — those are the point.
